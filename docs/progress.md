## Questions waiting for the user
- **Bars on a terminal that draws East Asian "ambiguous" characters two columns wide (a known limitation; needs your OK).** What you see: on a terminal set to draw ambiguous-width characters wide (an option some CJK setups turn on; most terminals draw them narrow by default), every progress bar of a known total is about twice as wide as counted and wraps, a row per update. The bar's blocks (U+2588 and the eighths) are ambiguous-width, and multiCMD counts them as one column, as 1.67 did (not worse in 1.68; found by 1.68's second opinions, from reading, not seen on such a terminal). Why not fix: a program cannot ask the terminal how it draws them; guessing from a CJK locale would be wrong for most CJK terminals, which draw them narrow, and an ASCII bar would change every bar for everyone. Revisit when: someone sees it on a real terminal. Options: **(1) known limitation, with one README line (recommended)** / (2) draw the bar in ASCII (`#`) under a CJK locale. Your words it touches: none. Waits on it: only that entry's text; 1.68 ships without it, as 1.67 did.
- **A bar drawn on past its total leaves its last frame's line open (a known limitation; needs your OK).** What you see: a program that draws several frames past a bar's total (99, then 101, 102 and 103 of 100) gets the first past frame's line ended in 1.69; the later frames draw on the next line and the last stays open, so the program's next output joins it (`...|103.0% | next`). 1.68 drew them all on one row and joined it the same way, so 1.69 adds one line holding the 101% frame. A second bar of the same total whose first frame is already past it gets no line end, as in 1.68. No hpcp bar passes its total: the final review and the fix round both read hpcp 9.96's counters and found none that can (not run on real hpcp jobs), so under hpcp 1.69 adds no line. Why not fix: only the caller knows which frame is its last; ending every frame at or past the total scrolls a line per frame for a program with an estimated total. Revisit when: a tool draws past its total in practice. Options: **(1) known limitation, with the README sentence corrected to say only the first frame past the total ends its line (recommended)** / (2) end every frame at or past the total (a line per frame). Your words it touches: none (the 1.68 deferral "a bar past its total ends its line" was a review's plan). Waits on it: only that entry's text; 1.69 can ship without it.
